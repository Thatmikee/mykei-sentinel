## Why it is built this way

A chaser fails when it repeats the first email louder. This one is not a nudge,
it is a different, smaller email that happens to reference the first.

Four things are doing the work:

1. **It shrinks the ask.** The first email asked for something broad. Broad asks
   need a decision, and undecided people do not reply. One hour of one person's
   time can be said yes to without a meeting.
2. **It gives them a free exit.** "A one line no is a complete answer" removes
   the cost of replying. Most silence is not rejection, it is the reply being
   more expensive than the ignore.
3. **It removes any commercial smell.** It states plainly that nothing is for
   sale and no deployments are running. A university that suspected a vendor
   approach now knows it is not one.
4. **It uses the alumnus link once, in passing.** Stated as fact, not leverage.

Do not add a deadline you do not have. An invented deadline is the one thing
that would make this read like the emails they already ignore.
